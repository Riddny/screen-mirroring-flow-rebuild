# Growth Audit — Screen Mirroring · Smart TV

**Live:** https://riddny.github.io/screen-mirroring-flow-rebuild/
**Prototype only:** https://riddny.github.io/screen-mirroring-flow-rebuild/?open=proto

Five ranked ideas for Screen Mirroring's iOS onboarding and paywall, each built into a clickable prototype in the app's own design. The screens are yours: the deep navy, the blue pill button, the thin progress bar, the TV-over-phone illustrations, the find-your-TV rings and logo tiles, and the pill plan cards with the Best price badge. Only the order changes, plus a few things you already own but do not show at the right moment.

| # | Idea | Screen | Potential outcome |
| :-- | :-- | :-- | :-- |
| 01 | Offer a free trial when they close the paywall: the same ₪149.90 yearly plan, with a reminder before it bills | Offer on close | ARPU +10–15% |
| 02 | Ask which TV they have, then name it on the paywall | Your TV · Paywall | CR +15–20% |
| 03 | Show 11M+ users and your 4.6 stars before the price | Slide + proof | CR +10–15% |
| 04 | Ask what they want on the big screen, then read it back | Big screen · Find your TV · Slide | CR +10–15% |
| 05 | Say how much yearly saves against weekly (88% less) | Paywall | CR +5–10% |

The prototype has a Light / Dark switch, English / Russian / Spanish, a 30-second walkthrough, and jump chips that land on any screen with answers filled in.

**Grounding.** Every price, figure and feature line comes from Screen Mirroring's own in-app screens, App Store listing and App Store screenshots. Impact ranges are Adapty's expected effect from teardowns and A/B tests across subscription apps — not measured lift for Screen Mirroring.
